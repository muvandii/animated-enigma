> A strategy in here is worth more than a strategy you are still hoping about.

not a feeling.

## Rules

1. **Kill criteria are decided before the run.** Write them in the thesis. If
   you invent new criteria after seeing the results, that is not a kill, that is
   a rescue.
2. **Every kill gets a failure note.** Same day. Template:
   `shared/notes-templates/failure-note-template.md`.
3. **"I could tune it more" is not a root cause.** Name the mechanism: costs ate
   it, the effect is not out-of-sample stable, the signal was look-ahead, the
   universe is survivorship-biased, the effect was a leverage artifact.
4. **Reanimation is allowed exactly once** per strategy, and only if the fix is
   a *new, pre-registered claim* — not a parameter search.

## Why this is graded

Most strategies you build will die. An honest graveyard with 12 entries proves
you ran the loop 12 times. A portfolio with 3 "winners" and no graveyard proves
you stopped at the first thing that looked good on a chart.
