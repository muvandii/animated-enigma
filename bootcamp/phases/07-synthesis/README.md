# Phase 07 — Synthesis (Week 13)

One week. No new strategies, no new code features. The deliverable is legibility.

## The test

A stranger clones your repo, runs two commands, and can answer:

1. What did you build?
2. What died, and why?
3. What survived, and by how much?
4. What would you do differently?
5. What do you still not know?

If any of those five is unanswerable from the repo alone, Week 13 is not done.

## Two temptations to resist

**Backfilling notes.** If you are short daily notes, record the shortfall and
take the hit. A note written in Week 13 about Week 4 is fiction, and the vault's
entire value is that it is contemporaneous.

**Writing the writeup you wish were true.** The strongest writeup in this course
is the one that says "one of sixteen strategies survived, here is the number, and
here is why I do not trust it either." That is a more impressive document than
any backtest, because almost nobody writes it.
